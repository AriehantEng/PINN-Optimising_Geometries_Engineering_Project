## How low can the pressure drop go? Re_h = 10

Finite elements (P2/P1, mesh max area 0.02, 3 corner refinements), parabolic inflow, parent 3 long, daughters 8 long, w1 + w2 = 2, every width >= 0.2. 'share' is the fraction of the flow daughter 1 must carry. 'drop' is the flux-weighted total-pressure drop (E_in - E_out1 - E_out2) / Q_in, in units of rho U^2. 'fine' re-solves the optimum on max area 0.005 with 5 refinements. 'friction law' is w1 / w0 = sqrt(q) / (sqrt(q) + sqrt(1 - q)).
